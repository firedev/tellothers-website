You change the world with what you truly want. When you do what you actually want, the universe starts helping you.

True desires come from the soul. False ones come from your head and the people around you. From ads. From other people’s success stories. From the fear of falling behind.

Do what you want — from the inside, not the outside “I should get some work done” kind.

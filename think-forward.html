Think forward

Where is the thought pointing?

Every thought points somewhere. Back, at grading what already happened. Or forward, at building a new reality.

Ask a thought what set it off and where it’s looking. True thoughts don’t start outside. The impulse comes from within.

Whatever pulls you back — fear, doubt, second thoughts — is a lie. A mind game. Real thoughts point forward and make reality better. That’s the nature of insight.

You can’t focus on what you want and what you don’t want at the same time.

Only you know what makes sense. Trust yourself.

Think forward — not from others, but for others.

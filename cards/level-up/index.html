Level up

From “all for me” to “all for all”

The idea of raising your level of consciousness runs through every religion and philosophy. A movement from “all for me” to “all for all”.

You can hear the level in how people talk. “Life sucks.” “My life sucks.” “I’m ok — you’re not” — most people live here, and this trap may never let go. “We’re ok — they’re not.” And the fifth: “everything’s good” — no fear, no stress, no conflict there.

To solve a problem, you rise one level above it.

You are the author of your reality, not a victim of circumstances.

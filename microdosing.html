Turn off the receiver

Not tuning. Breakthrough

Silicon Valley has used psychedelics for decades, going back to Steve Jobs. They clear out what the mind has been fed.

A microdose tunes the receiver. A big dose turns the receiver off, and you see what’s been broadcasting your whole life.

The first time I took mushrooms I had closed-eye visions, and I saw my whole life laid out. The whole picture. Everything at once, with you inside it.

Twenty years later I had five grams of dried mushrooms. I crumbled them, poured boiling water over them, and drank about three and a half. And all I saw in the dark were two insights:

  1. Everything is beautiful and glowing
  2. Lines of fate (a bit like in Slaughterhouse-Five)

It all made sense at once, because I already knew it; everything else simply fell away. I realized the first thing I should care about is me. That my whole life I’d been looking through the wrong end of the binoculars.

With mushrooms, one gram and four grams are different worlds. A different kind of experience altogether. Music sounds alien, and the euphoria turns spiritual.

I kept wondering: when the ego dissolves and you find yourself in a tiny universe, how am I seeing this? With what?

That’s what big doses show you. How reality and life are put together. That you live in the field rather than play in it. That there’s a space of possibilities. That maya can be pierced. I pierced it underwater on a retreat, without mushrooms, but mushrooms lead to the same place.

If it pulls you back into the negative, the psychedelic isn’t to blame. Something is missing in you. Fill yourself first.

The first time, take a big dose. With a guide. With intention. Microdoses come later, for everyday life. And what makes it stick is a clean head, a sober, disciplined mind. The strongest psychedelic there is.

With microdosing I tune myself to whatever I need. I reach states where I can’t understand why everyone doesn’t live like this. Some will call it addiction. Then we’re simply going different ways.

Not doing psychedelics is a crime. You forget what you’re alive for.

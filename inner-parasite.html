Mind

The inner parasite

It feeds on fear

Each one of us grows a brain parasite, and it is poisoning your whole life. Its name is you.

It feeds on fear. A “problem” is the thing we keep not solving, so the nervous system gets its habitual state. A self-fulfilling prophecy.

An inner thermostat: my life is 70% good, just because I decided so, and the other 30% is reserved for problems. When there are no real ones, you fill that space with whatever turns up, so it doesn’t sit idle.

The trick is — none of it exists. The unpleasant part is produced personally, in your head. It’s all imagined. You can simply stop paying attention.

That’s what enlightenment is.

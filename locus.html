Locus

The assemblage point

Locus is the spot you’re aware of yourself from. Castaneda called it the assemblage point.

Shift it from words to sensations, and you can tell true desires from borrowed ones.

An internal locus is the driver. Win: “I practiced.” Lose: “Next time, harder.”

An external one is the passenger. Win: “Got lucky.” Lose: “The universe is against me.”
